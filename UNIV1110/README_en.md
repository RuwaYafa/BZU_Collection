# UNIV1110 — Artificial Intelligence and Media Literacy

**الذكاء الاصطناعي والتربية الإعلامية**  
**Birzeit University — University Requirement Course**

UNIV1110 is a one-credit university requirement course designed primarily for first-year students. It develops foundational knowledge and practical competencies for understanding and using Artificial Intelligence responsibly while critically engaging with digital and media environments.

The course connects **AI literacy, responsible Generative AI use, academic integrity, ethics, algorithmic bias, critical thinking, media literacy, digital rights, privacy, and responsible digital content production**, with attention to the Palestinian context.

---

## 🎯 Course Learning Focus

Students develop competencies related to:

- Understanding fundamental AI concepts and Generative AI.
- Using Generative AI tools effectively and responsibly.
- Applying **Prompt Engineering** and evaluating AI-generated outputs.
- Recognizing **hallucination**, bias, and AI limitations.
- Understanding ethical principles, privacy, and responsible AI use.
- Applying academic integrity principles when using AI.
- Critically evaluating digital and media content.
- Understanding digital rights and data privacy.
- Producing responsible digital content in the Palestinian context.

## 🤖 Responsible AI Use

AI is approached as a tool that students should learn to **understand, question, verify, and use responsibly**.

Students remain responsible for the academic work they submit and for critically reviewing AI-generated content. Course activities emphasize human judgment, verification, transparency, academic integrity, privacy, and responsible use rather than uncritical dependence on AI tools.

## 🌱 Teaching Approach

Students participate in:

- Discussion and questioning
- Hands-on experimentation
- Verification and fact-checking
- Critical analysis
- Individual reflection
- Collaborative activities
- Responsible digital production

The aim is not merely to teach students **how to use AI tools**, but to develop the ability to understand their limitations, evaluate their outputs, recognize their social and ethical implications, and make informed decisions about their use.

## 🇵🇸 Context

The course gives particular attention to digital rights, representation, algorithmic bias, media literacy, and responsible production of digital content within the **Palestinian context**.

The final stages bring these competencies together through digital campaign design and responsible construction and communication of Palestinian narratives.
