# COMP233 – Discrete Mathematics 🎓
**Birzeit University | Faculty of Engineering and Technology | Department of Computer Science**  
**Spring 2026**

---

## 📘 Course Description
This course introduces students to ideas and techniques from discrete mathematics widely used in computer science — covering logic, proofs, sets, functions, relations, counting, graphs, and trees with emphasis on CS applications.

**Prerequisites:** MATH1411 – Calculus II  
**Textbook:** *Discrete Mathematics with Applications (4th Edition)* – Susanna S. Epp, Brooks/Cole
